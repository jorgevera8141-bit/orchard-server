# Changelog

## [1.0.2.0] - 2026-10-13

### Added
- The weather badge now shows the live conditions (sun, night, clouds, rain, storm, snow, fog) and its ring pulses gold at 88F and red at 90F.
- The heat banner slides in when it appears instead of jumping on screen, and field buttons give a small press response.

### Changed
- Block detail cards (irrigation, info, fertilizer) match the premium design: readable title, a tab bar instead of arrow buttons, labelled info rows and full-width actions.

## [1.0.1.0] - 2026-10-02

### Changed
- Refresh the admin and field interfaces with clearer hierarchy, roomier layouts, and improved small-screen behavior.
- Group field actions in a bottom dock and improve keyboard and screen-reader access across the app.
- Make login and admin controls easier to read and use.
